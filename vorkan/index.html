From green bean to your cup

How a Vorkan bag is made

Source

We choose green coffee from volcanic highlands in Ethiopia, Brazil and Sumatra, tasting samples before we buy.

Roast

Small batches, each with its own profile. Light roasts keep the fruit, dark roasts bring out the cocoa.

Rest & pack

Beans rest 24 hours to release CO₂, then go into valve bags stamped with the roast date.

Deliver

Ground to your order and shipped across Egypt, usually within 1 to 3 days.
